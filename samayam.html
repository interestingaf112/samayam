Our Story

Rooted in tradition,
brewed for today.

At സമയം (Samayam), which means 'Time' in Malayalam, we believe that good things shouldn't be rushed. We source our beans directly from the lush estates of Wayanad, ensuring every cup carries the essence of the monsoons and the warmth of the sun.

Our space is designed to be a sanctuary away from the hustle. A place to read, converse, or simply watch the world go by while enjoying artisanal bakes.
